How to make pixel art from a photo

1. Choose an image

Drop a photo or illustration onto the upload area, or select one from your device.

2. Set your style

Increase pixel size for chunkier blocks. Reduce color levels for a limited palette. Try contrast and warm tones.

3. Save a PNG

Compare with the original, then download the pixelated result as a PNG image.

Common questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. The image is read and processed in your browser. This version does not send image files to a server.

What size will the download be?

The output keeps your image proportions. Images larger than 2400 pixels on their longest side are reduced to 2400 pixels to keep editing responsive.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. Choose an image from your phone and use the same controls to create and save pixel art.
